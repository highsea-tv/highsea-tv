Brand direction

The official direction is Abyss Neutral with the Bone accent, locked on 16 Oct 2026. Its specification is in docs/brand/direction.md. The four original explorations below are kept as a record and are no longer maintained.

Official direction · v1.0

Abyss Neutral

The chosen direction. Neutral graphite surfaces, no glows, a white focus ring and a warm Bone accent. Layouts: Hero + rows (TV), Tabs + carousel (phone) and Sidebar + widgets (admin). Sounds are dry felt-and-wood taps with almost no tail.

Direction D

Jolly Roger

Leans into the name: “sailing the high seas.” Tarred black and parchment, with brass and lantern gold. Pirata One titles. The TV home is a ship’s manifest; the admin is a captain’s ledger with a bounty board. Optional Pirate English copy, plus bells, a concertina and a bosun’s pipe.
