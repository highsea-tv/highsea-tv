## 2026.11.19.9

- Four TV and anime indexers are on the Beta channel: EZTV (a show's episodes by its IMDb id), Nyaa (anime, by its words), and SubsPlease and showRSS (feeds of their newest episodes). They need highsea's indexer engine 1.2; an older server leaves them out until it updates.
- The automatic search leaves out Nyaa and SubsPlease for now, as anime counts its episodes in ways highsea can't place yet. Interactive search lists what Nyaa finds.

## 2026.11.19.2

- YTS: films at 720p, 1080p and 2160p from YTS's public API, found by their IMDb id when highsea knows it.
- Knaben: a search over many public trackers' films, from Knaben's public API.
- BitSearch: a torrent search engine's films. It allows 200 requests a day from one address, so it's used for searches only, not for checking new releases every 15 minutes.
- Torrents-CSV: an open list of seeded torrents, for searches only.
