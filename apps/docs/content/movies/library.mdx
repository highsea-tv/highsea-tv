Written for highsea 0.1.0.

**Movies** lists every film in the library, in a **Grid** of posters or a **Table**. The switch at the top of the page changes the view, and your browser remembers it.

![The Movies library as a grid of posters, with status tabs, filters and sort above it](/images/admin/movies/library-grid.png)

## Find films in the library

- **Status tabs**, each with a count: **All**, **Available**, **Wanted**, **Downloading** and **Not released**.
- **Filter by title**: narrows the list as you type.
- **Filter by tag**: shows films with one tag. It appears once you have tags, which are made in **Settings › Media management**.
- **Sort by**: **Title**, **Date added**, **Year** or **Rating**.

The list loads in pages; **Show more** at the bottom loads the next. Your status, filter and sort stay in the page's address, so Back returns to them.

A film's status is one of **Available**, **Wanted**, **Downloading**, **Not released** or **Not monitored**. In the grid, a film shows its status on the poster unless it's simply available.

![The Movies library as a table, one film to a row with its status and details](/images/admin/movies/library-table.png)

## Turn a filter into a collection

**Save as smart collection** opens a new collection with the current status, title filter, tag and sort as its first rules. See [Start from the library](/collections/make/#start-from-the-library).

## Change many films at once

Switch to **Table** and tick the films, or tick the box in the header to select every film shown. A bar appears with:

- **Search**: search your indexers for each selected film.
- **Edit**, a menu with:
  - **Monitor** and **Stop monitoring**;
  - **Quality profile**: moves each film's first version to the profile you choose; any extra versions keep theirs;
  - **Subtitle languages**;
  - **Add a tag** and **Remove a tag**, once you have tags.
- **Remove**: takes the films out of the library and stops monitoring them. Their files stay on disk. If you have import lists, you can also tick **Don't add them again from lists**.
- **Clear**: deselects everything.

![Two films ticked in the table, with the selection bar's Edit menu open](/images/admin/movies/library-selection.png)
