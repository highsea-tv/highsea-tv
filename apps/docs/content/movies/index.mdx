Written for highsea 0.2.0-beta.3.

Two pages in the admin's sidebar handle films. **Discover** finds a film and adds it. **Movies** is your library: what's here, what's still wanted, and the place to change many films at once. Every film, in the library or not, has its own page. Like the rest of the admin, they're for the owner and admins.

highsea reads titles, people and collections from TMDB, with its own key or yours. If Discover says "Add a TMDB key to find movies.", highsea has no key to use: add one in **Settings › General**, under Metadata. See [Metadata](/settings/general/metadata/).
