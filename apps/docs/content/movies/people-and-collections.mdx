Written for highsea 0.1.0.

## People

Select anyone in a film's cast or its **Cast and crew** tab to open their page: their portrait, when and where they were born, their biography, **Known for** (their best-known titles), then their **Filmography**.

![A person's page: their portrait, biography and Known for, then the Filmography with its filters](/images/admin/movies/person.png)

- Filter the filmography by **All**, **In the library** or **Not in the library**, and by department.
- A title in the library shows **In library**; others have **Add**, which opens the same add dialog. See [Add a film](/movies/discover/#add-a-film).

## A film's collection

When a film belongs to a series on TMDB, its **Overview** has a row for the collection: how many of its films are in the library, and a button such as **Add 3 missing** to add the rest in one go, with the same options for each.

![A film's Overview with the row for its TMDB collection: how many of its films are in the library, and the button to add the missing ones](/images/admin/movies/film-collection.png)

The collection's name opens its own page, with its overview and every film in it.

![A TMDB collection's page: its overview and every film in it](/images/admin/movies/collection.png)

These are TMDB's collections. To group films your own way, see [Collections](/collections/).
