Written for highsea 0.1.0.

A film's page is the same page whether the film is in the library or not. Not added, it shows what the film is, with **Add movie**:

- the summary: poster, runtime, genres, rating, tagline and who directed it;
- **Overview**: the cast, trailers, the collection it belongs to, **More like this**, and **About** (original title and language, certification, budget, box office, studios and links);
- **Cast and crew**: everyone credited, by department.

![Metropolis, not in the library: its summary with Add movie, and the Overview tab with its cast](/images/admin/movies/film-not-added.png)

Once added, the page gains the library's side: the status and a tag for each version under the title, a **Monitored** switch, **Search** and **Interactive search** at the top, and the **Files**, **History**, **Search** and **Subtitles** tabs.

![Sintel in the library, with its two versions under the title, the Monitored switch, Search, Interactive search and the page's tabs](/images/admin/movies/film.png)

## What's in the library, and what's downloading

On the **Overview** tab:

- **In the queue** appears while the film is downloading, with a card for each download: its release, state, progress, speed and time left, and the reason when one is stalled or blocked. [Activity](/activity/) has the whole queue.
- **In the library** lists its versions with their quality profiles, its subtitle languages, tags, minimum availability and root folder.

## Search for the film now

Press **Search**. highsea searches your indexers in the background and grabs the best release your quality profile accepts, for each version that still wants a file or an upgrade. Follow it in [Activity](/activity/). With no indexer enabled, **Search** is unavailable and says why.

## Choose a release yourself

Press **Interactive search**, or open the **Search** tab. It lists every release your indexers offer, with its quality, score, size, peers and age, and whether your profile **Accepted** or **Rejected** it.

![The Search tab: releases from the indexers with their quality, score, size, peers and age, and whether the profile accepted them](/images/admin/movies/film-search.png)

- Opening the tab again soon after shows the last results rather than asking every indexer again. **Search again** always searches.
- A rejected release shows how many reasons it has; select the badge to read them.
- **Show rejected** hides or shows the releases your profile turned down.
- The download button grabs a release. On a rejected one it's **Grab anyway**, which overrides your profile.
- If an indexer didn't answer, the tab says which and why.

## Files

The **Files** tab has a card for each version: its file's size and path, each video, audio and subtitle stream on its own line, and the release name. A version without a file says **No file yet**, or **Downloading**.

![The Files tab: a card for each of Sintel's versions, with the file's size and path and its streams](/images/admin/movies/film-files.png)

## History

The **History** tab lists what happened to the film, newest first: grabs, imports, failed downloads and imports, renames, deletions, blocklistings, and subtitles downloaded, synced, upgraded or deleted. **Show more** loads older events.

![The History tab: Sintel's events, newest first](/images/admin/movies/film-history.png)

## Subtitles

The **Subtitles** tab lists the film's subtitle tracks, files beside the film and streams inside it, with their language, where each came from, its score, whether it's synced and its offset.

- **Offset**: the − and + buttons show a track 50 ms earlier or later.
- **Sync to the audio again** re-syncs a track to the film's speech.
- **Delete** removes a track.
- **Search for subtitles**: choose a language and press **Search providers**, then **Use** on a result to download it. It's synced in the background.

![The Subtitles tab: Sintel's tracks with their language, source, score, sync state and offset, and Search for subtitles below](/images/admin/movies/film-subtitles.png)

Tracks stored as pictures have nothing to move, so they have no offset or re-sync. Searching needs the film's file, and a subtitle provider turned on. See [Subtitles](/subtitles/).
