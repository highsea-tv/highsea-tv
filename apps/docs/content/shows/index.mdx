Written for highsea 0.4.9.

**Shows** in the admin's sidebar lists the shows in your Shows folders. highsea reads them where they are: it matches each show folder to a show on TMDB, and each file to the episodes it holds by its name (`S01E01`, `S01E01-E02`, `1x01`, or `01 - Title` inside a `Season 01` folder). It doesn't rename, move or download episodes yet.

To add a Shows folder and import what's in it, see [Root folders](/settings/media-management/root-folders/). After that, highsea scans the folder every 12 hours and when something in it changes.

## A show's page

Open a show to see its seasons, each with its episodes, its air date and its file. **Files** lists every file once, with the episodes it holds. The top of the page is the show's backdrop, with its logo, its details and **Monitored** over it.

![A show's page: its backdrop with the logo and details over it, and its first season open below](/images/admin/shows/show.png)

The More menu (**More actions**) has the same items as a film's, in the same order: **Edit**, **Change artwork**, **Refresh metadata** and **Remove from library**.

### Change a show's artwork

Open the More menu and choose **Change artwork**. It's the dialog a film has ([Change a film's artwork](/movies/manage-a-film/#change-a-films-artwork)): a tab each for **Poster**, **Backdrop** and **Logo**, with **From TMDB** first and, when fanart.tv is on, **From fanart.tv** after it, or **Upload** your own. Your choice stays through every refresh until you reset it, and every app shows it. A season keeps TMDB's poster for now.

![The Artwork dialog for Chernobyl on the Poster tab, with TMDB's posters](/images/admin/shows/show-artwork.png)

### Refresh a show's details

Open the More menu (**More actions**) and choose **Refresh metadata**. highsea asks TMDB for the show, its seasons and its episodes again, and says **Refreshing from TMDB**. New episodes and changed details show on the page when TMDB answers. A file kept as unmatched because TMDB didn't list its episode is tried again at the next scan.

![A show's More menu, open, with Edit, Change artwork, Refresh metadata and Remove from library](/images/admin/shows/show-more.png)

## Subtitles for episodes

Episodes get subtitles the way films do, from the same providers and settings on [Subtitles](/subtitles/):

- **Found in place:** subtitles inside the file, and subtitle files named after the episode's file, beside it or in the season folder's `Subs` folder. A season folder holds many episodes, so highsea uses only the ones named after that episode.
- **Downloaded:** OpenSubtitles.com, Subdl and SubSource are asked for that show's episode by its season and number. A subtitle for another episode, or for a whole season, isn't used.
- **Languages:** a show takes the subtitle languages of the root folder it's in, the folder's **Subtitle languages for movies added here** setting. A show that has none gets no subtitles.

The players offer an episode's subtitles as they do a film's. The admin doesn't list an episode's subtitles yet, and the **Wanted** list on Subtitles shows films only.

## The space shows take

The dashboard's **Storage** widget lists your Shows folders beside your films', each with how many shows it holds. **System › Status** shows what uses each disk, with **Shows** counted on its own.
