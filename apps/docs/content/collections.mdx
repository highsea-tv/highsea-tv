Written for highsea 0.1.0.

A collection groups films your way. Its films come from **rules** that keep themselves up to date, such as "from the 2010s, rated 4.5 or more, in English", or from films you **pick by hand**, such as "Staff picks". Collections are made for the people who watch, and a collection can also feed a featured section on the home screen; see [Curation](/curation/).

**Collections** is in the admin's sidebar, below Movies, for Owners and Admins.

## The list of collections

Each collection has a card with:

- its artwork and name;
- **Smart** if it has rules, or **Picked** if its films were chosen by hand;
- its rules in words, such as "Released 2010–2020, rated 4.5 or more, in English";
- what it holds and who sees it, such as "4 films, 3 in the apps", or "Nothing matches yet";
- **Edit** and **Delete**.

Only films with a file show in the apps. A collection whose films match but none has a file yet is marked **Hidden in the apps**.

## Make a collection

Press **New collection**. On the left is the form; beside it, **Right now** shows what the collection holds as you change it: how many films match, how many have a file and show in the apps, and their posters in the collection's order. A film without a file is dimmed.

1. Give it a **Name**. The apps show it.
2. Add a **Summary** if you like, shown under the name.
3. Under **Artwork**, keep **The mosaic** of the first four posters, or choose one film's poster.
4. Under **How films get in**, choose **Use rules** or **Pick them**.
5. Set the **Order** and **Who sees it**.
6. Press **Save collection**.

You can also start from the library: on **Movies**, filter and sort the list, then press **Save as smart collection**. The new collection starts with the status, title filter, tag and sort you had as its rules. See [Discover and Movies](/movies/).

## Use rules

A rule is a condition: a field, a comparison and a value, such as **Year** **between** 2010 and 2020, or **Genre** **includes any of** Animation.

- Choose **Match all of these conditions** or **Match any of these conditions** at the top.
- **Add a condition** adds another row. The remove button beside a row takes it away.
- **Add a group** adds a group of conditions with its own all or any, for rules like "all of these, and any of those". Groups go one level deep.
- With no conditions, every film is in it: "No conditions yet, so every film is in it. Add one to narrow it down."

If a condition isn't finished when you save, it's marked and the form says "Finish the conditions marked below."

### The fields

The fields come in groups. The choices offered, such as genres, languages, studios, tags and people, are the ones your library has, so every choice can match something.

| Group | Fields |
| --- | --- |
| Release | Year, Release date, Decade |
| Ratings | Rating (out of 5 stars), Votes, Certification |
| Language | Original language, Audio language, Subtitle language |
| Content | Title, Genre, Runtime, Keyword, Studio, Country, In a collection |
| People | Actor, Director, Writer, Composer, Producer, Cinematographer, Editor, Anyone in the cast and crew |
| Library | Date added, Status, Tag, Root folder, Quality profile |
| File | Resolution, HDR, Video codec, Edition, Size |

**Watched** and **Last played** are listed but can't be used yet; they're marked "comes with playback".

A few things worth knowing:

- **In the last** keeps a rule current: "Year in the last 2 years" or "Date added in the last 30 days" moves forward with the calendar.
- **People fields match one role.** "Composer includes" matches only films that person scored, not ones they act in. **Anyone in the cast and crew** matches any role. Choosing several people matches a film with any of them.
- **In a collection** matches films in one of TMDB's collections, or in another of your own.

## Pick films by hand

Choose **Pick them**, then **Search the library** and **Add** each film. Drag the handles to put them in order. A picked collection needs at least one film.

Switching from **Use rules** to **Pick them** starts the picks with the films the rules found, in their order. Switch back before saving and your rules return.

## Exceptions

With **Use rules**, two lists adjust what the rules find:

- **Always include**: in it whatever the rules say.
- **Never include**: left out whatever the rules say.

A film can be in only one of the two.

## Order and how many

- **Sort by**: **Rating**, **Release date**, **Year**, **Date added**, **Title**, **Runtime**, **Popularity** or **Shuffled, once a day**. A picked collection can also keep **In your order**.
- **Direction**, in the field's own words: **Highest first**, **Newest first**, **A to Z** and so on.
- **How many**: leave it empty for every film, or enter a number to keep only the first ones in that order.

**Shuffled, once a day** gives a new order each day, and the same order all day, so a row doesn't reshuffle on every visit.

## Who sees it

**Who sees it** sets the collection's audience: **Everyone**, or only some **Roles**, some **People** or some kinds of **Apps**.

## How collections keep up

A smart collection isn't a saved list of films. highsea works out what it holds each time it's shown, so it follows the library as films arrive, leave or change: a new film that matches is in it at once, and a film you remove is gone.

A collection with nothing to show isn't shown in the apps until a film in it has a file.

## Change or delete a collection

Press **Edit** on its card, change what you need, and press **Save changes**.

**Delete**, on the card or in the editor, asks first. The collection leaves every app's library; the films in it stay in your library. A collection that a featured section draws on can't be deleted until you choose another source for that section in [Curation](/curation/).

## TMDB's collections

A film that belongs to a series on TMDB, such as a trilogy, shows that collection on its page, with a button to add the films you're missing. Those come from TMDB and can't be edited here. See [Discover and Movies](/movies/).
