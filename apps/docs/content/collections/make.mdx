Written for highsea 0.1.0.

## The editor

On **Collections**, press **New collection**. On the left is the form; beside it, **Right now** shows what the collection holds as you change it: how many films match, how many have a file and show in the apps, and their posters in the collection's order. A film without a file is dimmed.

![The collection editor for Open movies of the 2010s: name, summary and artwork, its rules under Films, Exceptions, Order and Who sees it, with Right now beside them showing 2 films that match and 1 with a file](/images/admin/collections/rules.png)

1. Give it a **Name**. The apps show it.
2. Add a **Summary** if you like, shown under the name.
3. Under **Artwork**, keep **The mosaic** of the first four posters, or choose one film's poster.
4. Under **Films**, choose **Use rules** (see [Use rules](/collections/rules/)) or **Pick them** (see below).
5. Set the **Order** and **Who sees it**.
6. Press **Save collection**.

## Start from the library

On **Movies**, filter and sort the list, then press **Save as smart collection**. The new collection starts with the status, title filter, tag and sort you had as its rules. See [The library](/movies/library/#turn-a-filter-into-a-collection).

## Pick films by hand

Choose **Pick them**, then **Search the library** and **Add** each film. Drag the handles to put them in order. A picked collection needs at least one film.

![Blender picks in the editor with Pick them chosen: Sintel and Nosferatu listed with drag handles, a search box above them, and the order set to In your order](/images/admin/collections/picked.png)

Switching from **Use rules** to **Pick them** starts the picks with the films the rules found, in their order. Switch back before saving and your rules return.

## Exceptions

With **Use rules**, two lists adjust what the rules find:

- **Always include**: in it whatever the rules say.
- **Never include**: left out whatever the rules say.

A film can be in only one of the two.

## Order and how many

- **Sort by**: **Rating**, **Release date**, **Year**, **Date added**, **Title**, **Runtime**, **Popularity** or **Shuffled, once a day**. A picked collection can also keep **In your order**.
- **Direction**, in the field's own words: **Highest first**, **Newest first**, **A to Z** and so on.
- **How many**: leave it empty for every film, or enter a number to keep only the first ones in that order.

**Shuffled, once a day** gives a new order each day, and the same order all day, so a row doesn't reshuffle on every visit.

## Who sees it

**Who sees it** sets the collection's audience: **Everyone**, or only some **Roles**, some **People** or some kinds of **Apps**.

## Change or delete a collection

Press **Edit** on its card, change what you need, and press **Save changes**.

**Delete**, on the card or in the editor, asks first.

![The dialog that asks to delete Blender picks, saying it leaves every app's library and the films in it stay in the library](/images/admin/collections/delete.png)

 The collection leaves every app's library; the films in it stay in your library. A collection that a featured section draws on can't be deleted until you choose another source for that section in [Featured sections](/curation/featured-sections/#change-or-delete-a-section).
