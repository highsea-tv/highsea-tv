Written for highsea 0.4.0.

The highsea app for Apple TV has Home, the library, search and the player, drawn for a television and moved around with the remote. It finds the highsea servers on your network by itself, and you sign it in with your phone, so nobody types a password with a remote.

## Getting the app

The app isn't on the App Store or TestFlight yet. Everything on this page applies once it's available.

## Signing in

The app looks for highsea servers on your network as it opens. When it finds exactly one, it goes straight to signing in to it. When it finds several, **Servers on this network** lists them; choose yours. A server on another network is added by address from [the highsea app on your phone](/watch/iphone/).

### With your phone

**Sign in with your phone** shows a code and a QR code:

1. Scan the QR code with your phone's camera, or go to the address it shows in a browser on your phone or computer, already signed in to the same server.
2. Check the code matches, and approve this TV. With the iPhone app you can also use **Sign in a TV**, as [its guide](/watch/iphone/#signing-in-a-tv) says.

The TV says **Approved. Signing in…** and opens Home. A code works for 10 minutes; when one runs out, the TV shows a new one by itself.

### With a password

**Use a password** asks for your highsea username and password, typed on the keys on screen. If your account has two-step verification, it then asks for the 6-digit code from your authenticator app. Two-step verification is set up on the server's sign-in page in a browser, not on the TV.

The TV stays signed in until you sign it out, here or from **Settings › Your devices** on any of your devices.

## Finding your way with the remote

- **The touch surface or the arrows** move between things. The page and its rows scroll to follow.
- **Select (OK)** opens or chooses what's highlighted.
- **Back** (the **Menu** or **‹** button) goes back a step. On Home, it leaves highsea.
- **Play/Pause** plays the highlighted film from wherever you are, or pauses and resumes the one playing.
- **Home**, **Search**, **Library** and **Settings** stay at the top of every screen but the player. **Up** from the top of a page reaches them.

Each key plays a short sound, which you can turn off or quieten in **Settings › Sounds**.

## Watching

- **Home** shows a film at the top, with **Play** or **Resume** and **Details**; it changes to whichever film you rest on in the first row. **Continue watching**, **Recently added** and the rows the server's owner set up follow below.
- **Library** lists every film, sorted **A–Z**, **Recently added** or **Newest**, with **Unwatched** to show only what you haven't seen. **Collections** lists the server's collections.
- **Search** has keys on the left and results on the right, which change as you type. **&123** switches to numbers and symbols.
- **A film's page** has **Play** or **Resume**, **From the start**, and **Audio and subtitles**, which sets its version, sound and subtitles for all your devices. Hold Select on any of these for **Mark as watched** or **Mark as unwatched**.

### The player

While a film plays, any key shows the controls, and they hide again after a few seconds.

- **Left** and **Right** move back or forward 10 seconds. A preview of that point in the film shows above the bar once highsea has made the film's previews.
- **Select** or **Play/Pause** pauses and plays.
- **Down** reaches **Audio and subtitles**. With a subtitle on, **Subtitle timing** moves it by 50 ms at a time when it doesn't match the voices, and the timing is saved for that film.
- **Back** hides the controls, and with them hidden, leaves the film. highsea remembers where you were, on all your devices.

In a film's last seconds, **Next up** offers the next film in its collection, and starts it when the count reaches zero. **Cancel** lets the film end.

If the server is already making as many films as it's set to, the player says so; try again when one finishes. If the server's owner stops your film from [Now playing](/now-playing/), the player says so too, and **Start again** picks up where you were.

## Settings

- **Theme** sets how the app looks, with a preview of each theme's colours. **Use on all my devices** makes it your theme everywhere.
- **Sounds** turns the remote's sounds on or off and sets their volume.
- **Home screen** shows, hides and reorders the rows on Home, on every device.
- **Servers** says which server and account this TV uses, with **Another server** and **Sign out**.
- **Your devices** lists where you're signed in, and signs any of them out.
- **About** has the app's and the server's versions.

## When highsea can't reach your server

The app says **Can’t reach** and your server's name. **Try again** looks once more, **Another server** goes back to the list, and **Forget this server** signs this TV out of it and stops listing it.
