Written for highsea 0.4.0.

Every highsea server has a web app for watching, at `/web` on the same address as the admin: `http://<your server>:3000/web/` at home, or your remote address followed by `/web/` away from it. Nothing else needs installing. It's where Users watch, and Owners and Admins can watch there too.

## Signing in

Open `/web` and sign in with your highsea username and password, or with the single sign-on button when the server has one. If you've turned on two-step verification, it asks for your code next. Signing in on `/web` or on the admin's own sign-in page works the same way: a User who signs in on the admin's page lands on `/web`, and an Owner or Admin lands on the admin.

From the admin, **Watch on highsea**, the last item in the sidebar and in the command palette, opens `/web` in the same browser, already signed in.

**Change password** and **Set up a passkey** in the web app's **Account** are held off for now. Passwords, passkeys and two-step verification are changed on the server's sign-in page.

## Home

![Home in the web app: the Staff picks row by Ines and Blender night, under the top bar with the server's name, Search, Home, Collections and Movies, alerts and the account](/images/admin/watch/home.png)

**Home** opens with the server's featured film when there is one, then **Continue watching** with what you've started, then the rows the Owner set up in [Curation](/curation/) and **Recently added**. **Settings › Home screen** lets you reorder and hide rows; your order follows you to every device.

The top bar has the server's name at the start, with **Connected remotely** under it when you're away from home. In the middle are **Search**, **Home**, **Collections** and **Movies**. At the end, the bell opens **Alerts**, and your initial opens **Account**, **Downloads**, **Settings** and signing out.

## The library and search

![Movies in the web app: the filters All movies, All genres and Title, the letters down the side, and Nosferatu and Sintel as posters](/images/admin/watch/library.png)

**Movies** lists every movie you can watch, with **Show** (**All movies** or **Unwatched**), **Genre** and **Sort**. The letters down the side jump to titles starting with each. **Collections** has the Owner's collections and the ones films here belong to. **Search** finds titles, genres and people on the server.

## A film's page

![Sintel's page: its year, length, rating, genres and quality, the synopsis, Starring and Directed by, the line 1080p · Default audio · Subtitles off, and Play with the Trailer, Audio and subtitles and Watched buttons](/images/admin/watch/film.png)

A film's page has **Play**, or **Resume** and **Play from the start** once you've started it. Beside it, **Trailer** plays the film's trailer when it has one, and **Watched** marks it as watched or unwatched. Below come **Cast and crew**, the collections it's in, and **More like this**.

The line above **Play** says what it plays with. **Audio and subtitles** changes the **Version**, **Audio** and **Subtitles** before you play. What you choose is remembered for this movie, on all your devices, and the player starts with it. A version still waiting for its file isn't offered.

## Which browsers

The web app plays in current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari. highsea checks which formats your browser can play the first time you press Play, and again after the browser updates. A file the browser plays as it is, it gets as it is; otherwise the server repackages or converts it as it plays, which the player shows as **Getting it ready** for a moment.

A browser can't keep films for watching offline. **Downloads** explains that the desktop app does, with a link to get it.
