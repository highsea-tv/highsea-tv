Written for highsea 0.2.0-beta.1.

Every highsea server has a web app for watching, at `/web` on the same address as the admin: `http://<your server>:3000/web/` at home, or your remote address followed by `/web/` away from it. Nothing else needs installing. It's where Users watch, and Owners and Admins can watch there too.

## Signing in

Open `/web` and sign in with your highsea username and password, or with the single sign-on button when the server has one. If you've turned on two-step verification, it asks for your code next. Signing in on `/web` or on the admin's own sign-in page works the same way: a User who signs in on the admin's page lands on `/web`, and an Owner or Admin lands on the admin.

From the admin, **Watch on highsea**, the last item in the sidebar and in the command palette, opens `/web` in the same browser, already signed in.

Passwords, passkeys and two-step verification are changed on the server's sign-in page for now, as **Profile** in the web app says.

## Home

![Home in the web app: the Staff picks row by Ines, Blender night and Recently added, under the menu with Home, Search, Library and Downloads](/images/admin/watch/home.png)

**Home** opens with the server's featured film when there is one, then **Continue watching** with what you've started, then the rows the Owner set up in [Curation](/curation/) and **Recently added**. **Settings › Home screen** lets you reorder and hide rows; your order follows you to every device.

At the top, the server's name opens a short menu with the address in use. Away from home it says **Connected remotely**. Your initial, at the right, opens **Profile**, **Settings** and signing out.

## The library and search

![Library in the web app: Movies and Collections tabs, the filters All movies, All genres and Title, and Nosferatu and Sintel as posters](/images/admin/watch/library.png)

**Library** lists every movie you can watch, with **Show** (**All movies** or **Unwatched**), **Genre** and **Sort**. **Collections** beside it has the Owner's collections and the ones films here belong to. **Search** finds titles, genres and people on the server.

## A film's page

![Sintel's page: its year, length, genres and rating, the synopsis, Directed by Colin Levy, the Play button and More, and Watch with showing Version, Audio and Subtitles](/images/admin/watch/film.png)

A film's page has **Play**, or **Resume** and **Play from the start** once you've started it. **More** marks it as watched or unwatched.

**Watch with** picks the **Version**, **Audio** and **Subtitles** before you play. What you choose is remembered for this movie, on all your devices, and the player starts with it. A version still waiting for its file isn't offered.

## Which browsers

The web app plays in current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari. highsea checks which formats your browser can play the first time you press Play, and again after the browser updates. A file the browser plays as it is, it gets as it is; otherwise the server repackages or converts it as it plays, which the player shows as **Getting it ready** for a moment.

A browser can't keep films for watching offline. **Downloads** explains that this comes with the desktop app.
