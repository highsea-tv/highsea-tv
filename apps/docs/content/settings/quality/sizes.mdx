Written for highsea 0.1.0.

**Settings › Quality sizes** sets how large a release may be for its quality, in MB per minute of the film's runtime. A release outside the range is rejected.

![Settings › Quality sizes, a table of each quality with its minimum, preferred and maximum size per minute and what that means for a 2-hour film](/images/admin/settings-quality/sizes.png)

| Column | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Min** | The smallest a release may be. |
| **Preferred** | Between two otherwise equal releases, the one nearer this size wins. Empty, no preference. |
| **Max** | The largest a release may be. Empty, no limit. |
| **A 2-hour film** | What the range means for a two-hour film. |

MB here is a mebibyte (1,048,576 bytes), as in Radarr, so its numbers carry over. A film whose runtime isn't known is taken as 110 minutes.

The starting sizes come from the TRaSH Guides: a minimum for the HD and UHD qualities, and no preferred size or maximum, leaving custom formats to choose between releases. Set a maximum if your disks are small.

## Editing sizes

**Edit sizes** opens the editor. **Show sizes for a film of** changes the film length the last column uses, which helps when you think in sizes for a whole film. **Reset to TRaSH's sizes** puts the starting sizes back; save to keep them.

![The Edit sizes page, with Show sizes for a film of set to 120 minutes and a Min, Preferred and Max field for each quality](/images/admin/settings-quality/sizes-editor.png)
