Written for highsea 0.2.0-beta.3.

Where film details come from, and the language and region they're in. highsea gets titles, posters, cast and release dates from TMDB, with its own key unless you add yours. Only the Owner can change these.

![The Metadata group in Settings › General: the TMDB key shown as Set with Replace and Remove, then Metadata language and Region.](/images/admin/settings-general/metadata.png)

## The TMDB key

The key shows as **Set**, with its last four characters, when you've added your own. Otherwise it shows **highsea’s key**: highsea's own key is in use, and everything works without one of yours. A key of your own wins over highsea's.

highsea's key is built into its published image. If you built highsea yourself without one, the key shows **Not set**, and Discover, adding films and refreshing their details don't work until you add yours; **System › Health** says so. The library and its pages keep working.

To add or change your key:

1. Copy your key from [TMDB's API settings](https://www.themoviedb.org/settings/api). Either the **API Key** or the **API Read Access Token** works.
2. With no key set, paste it into **New TMDB key**. With one set, choose **Replace** first.
3. Choose **Save**. highsea checks the key with TMDB before it keeps it. If TMDB refuses it, copy it again; if TMDB can't be reached, nothing is saved, so check the server's internet connection and try again.

**Remove** takes your key away after you confirm, and highsea goes back to its own.

## Language and region

| Setting | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Metadata language** | The language of titles, overviews and taglines, where TMDB has them in it. | Your browser's language when you ran setup |
| **Region** | Used for certifications, release dates and Discover's lists. | Your browser's region when you ran setup |
