Written for highsea 0.1.0.

Everyone who opens the admin has **Your account**, at the bottom of the sidebar. It holds your name and email, your password, two-step verification and passkeys.

![Your account, open beside the admin: Profile with Name and an optional Email, and Password with Current password, New password of at least 10 characters, and Change password](/images/admin/settings-users-and-security/account.png)

## Your password

To change your password, open **Your account**, enter your **Current password** and the new one, and choose **Change password**. Your other browsers are signed out.

If you've forgotten it, ask the Owner for a [reset link](/settings/users-and-security/people/#reset-a-password). The Owner's own password can't be reset by anyone else.

## Two-step verification

Two-step verification asks for a 6-digit code from an authenticator app after your password. It's only asked for in the admin; apps never ask for it.

![Two-step verification in Your account: Off, signing in asks for your password only, the server's owner requires it, and Set up](/images/admin/settings-users-and-security/account-two-step.png)

To turn it on for your account:

1. Open **Your account** and choose **Set up** under **Two-step verification**.
2. Enter your password.
3. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app, or enter the key shown below it.
4. Enter the code the app shows and choose **Turn on**.
5. Save the 10 recovery codes. Each signs you in once if you lose your authenticator, and they show only now.

![Setting up two-step verification: a QR code to scan, the key to type instead, a Code field and Turn on](/images/admin/settings-users-and-security/two-step-setup.png)

**Your account** shows how many recovery codes you have left. **New recovery codes** replaces them all. At sign-in, **Use a recovery code** takes one instead of a code from the app.

When someone has lost both their authenticator and their recovery codes, the Owner can choose **Turn off two-step** from the **⋯** menu beside them in **Settings › Users and devices**. Make sure it's really them asking.

## Passkeys

A passkey signs you in with your fingerprint, face or device PIN, with no password to type. In **Your account**, choose **Add a passkey** and follow your browser. Each one shows whether it's **Synced** across your devices or kept **On one device**, and can be renamed or removed.

![Passkeys in Your account, with none yet and Add a passkey](/images/admin/settings-users-and-security/account-passkeys.png)

Passkeys work only when the admin is opened at its HTTPS address or at `localhost`. [Settings › Network and remote access](/settings/network/) covers HTTPS. At sign-in, choose **Sign in with a passkey**.
