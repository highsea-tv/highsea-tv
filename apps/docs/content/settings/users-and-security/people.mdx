Written for highsea 0.1.0.

The **People** tab of **Settings › Users and devices** lists everyone on the server, with their role, their libraries, whether they use two-step verification, and when they were last seen. Only the Owner can change anything here; see [Roles](/settings/users-and-security/#roles).

![The People tab: Ines, the Owner, marked You; Mara, a User, Invited and hasn't joined yet; Theo, a User with Movies; and Ruth, an Admin. Each but Ines has Edit and a more menu, and Invite someone sits at the top](/images/admin/settings-users-and-security/people.png)

## Invite someone

1. Go to **Settings › Users and devices** and choose **Invite someone**.
2. Enter their **Name**, as the household knows them.
3. Optionally enter their **Email**. If the server's mail is set up in [Connections](/settings/notifications/services/#the-servers-mail), the link is emailed to them as well.
4. Choose a **Role**: Admin or User. For a User, choose their **Libraries**: **All, now and later**, or pick from the list.
5. Choose **Create invite link**, then **Copy link** and send it to them.

![The Invite someone dialog, with Name, an optional Email, Role set to User, Libraries set to All, now and later, and Create invite link](/images/admin/settings-users-and-security/invite.png)

The link works once, for 7 days. Opening it, they choose a username and a password of at least 10 characters, then **Finish my account**. If single sign-on is on, they can choose **Join with** your provider instead, which links their account to it.

Until they finish, they show as **Invited** in the list, with "Hasn't joined yet". From the **⋯** menu beside them:

- **Copy the invite link** shows the link again, or a fresh one if the old one has expired.
- **Cancel the invite** stops the link working. You can invite them again later.

![The more menu beside Mara, who is invited, open on Copy the invite link and Cancel the invite](/images/admin/settings-users-and-security/invite-menu.png)

## Change what someone can do

Choose **Edit** beside a person to change their **Role**.

- Admins see every library and need nothing ticked.
- A User also has **Libraries** and **What they can do**: **Ask for titles** and **Download for offline**, which apply in highsea's apps rather than the admin. Changing the role resets these to the role's defaults.

![Theo's edit panel: Role is User, Ask for titles ticked and Download for offline not, and Libraries set to Choose with Movies ticked](/images/admin/settings-users-and-security/edit.png)

Making an Admin a User signs them out of the admin, and their API keys stop working.

The Owner's role can't be changed, and there's no way to hand the server to someone else in highsea 0.1.0.

## Reset a password

Anyone who has forgotten their password asks the Owner. In **Settings › Users and devices**, open the **⋯** menu beside them and choose **Make a password reset link**. Send them the link: it works once, for 7 days, and lets them choose a new password. It's emailed too when the server's mail is set up and they have an email address.

![The more menu beside Theo, open on Make a password reset link and Remove](/images/admin/settings-users-and-security/person-menu.png)

The Owner's own password can only be changed by the Owner, in [Your account](/settings/users-and-security/your-account/#your-password). Nobody else can reset it, so keep it somewhere safe, along with your recovery codes.

## Remove someone

From the **⋯** menu, choose **Remove**. They're signed out everywhere and can't sign back in, and their watch history goes with them. This can't be undone; invite them again if you change your mind.
