Written for highsea 0.1.0.

Three places decide who gets in and how:

- **Settings › Users and devices**: the people on the server, their roles, the browsers they're signed in on, and API keys.
- **Settings › Security**: the rules for signing in, such as two-step verification, passkeys and single sign-on. Only the Owner sees it.
- **Your account**: your own name, password, two-step verification and passkeys. Everyone has one; open it from the sidebar.

## Roles

Everyone on the server has one of three roles.

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything, including Security, Backups, VPN, Download client and Migration. The person who ran setup. There is one Owner. |
| **Admin** | Runs the server: the library, downloads, indexers and settings. Sees people and devices, but can't invite or change anyone. |
| **User** | Watches in the browser at `/web`, and in highsea's apps as they arrive. Signing in on the admin takes them to `/web`. |

Only the Owner invites people, changes their roles and removes them. An Admin opening **Settings › Users and devices** sees the same lists with the note "Only the owner can invite or change people."
