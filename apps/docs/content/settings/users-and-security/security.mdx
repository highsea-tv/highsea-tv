Written for highsea 0.1.0.

**Settings › Security** sets the rules for signing in to the server. Only the Owner sees this area. Changes take effect when you choose **Save**.

![Settings › Security: Signing in, with Require two-step for the owner and admins and Allow passkeys on, Stay signed in for 30 days and Trust a sign-in header from my proxy off; below it Single sign-on (OIDC), off](/images/admin/settings-users-and-security/security.png)

## Signing in

| Setting | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Require two-step for the owner and admins** | Owners and Admins must use [two-step verification](/settings/users-and-security/your-account/#two-step-verification) to open the admin. Anyone who hasn't set it up is asked to at their next sign-in, and **Turn off** disappears from their account. | On |
| **Allow passkeys** | Offers **Sign in with a passkey**, and lets people add [passkeys](/settings/users-and-security/your-account/#passkeys) in **Your account**. | On |
| **Stay signed in for** | How long a sign-in to the admin lasts: 1, 7, 30 or 90 days. | 30 days |
| **Trust a sign-in header from my proxy** | For Authelia, Authentik and other forward-auth proxies. The proxy signs people in and passes their username in a **Header**, `Remote-User` unless you change it. It only signs in existing accounts, and highsea believes it only from the trusted proxies listed in [Settings › Network and remote access](/settings/network/). | Off |

## Single sign-on (OIDC)

Lets people sign in with Authentik, Authelia, Keycloak or another OpenID Connect provider. It needs the server's external address, set in [Settings › Network and remote access](/settings/network/), so the provider can send people back.

1. In your provider, create a client for highsea.
2. In highsea, turn on **Offer single sign-on**.
3. Enter a **Button label**: the sign-in page shows "Sign in with" and this name.
4. Enter the **Client ID**, the **Issuer URL** (the provider's `https://` address) and, if your provider gives one, the **Client secret**. The secret is stored encrypted; leave the field blank to keep the stored one.
5. Copy the **Redirect URL** highsea shows into your provider, then **Save**.

![Single sign-on turned on: Button label, Client ID, Issuer URL and Client secret, and under Redirect URL a note that single sign-on needs the server's external URL, with Open Network](/images/admin/settings-users-and-security/single-sign-on.png)

Single sign-on doesn't create accounts. Each person joins through their [invite link](/settings/users-and-security/people/#invite-someone) with **Join with** your provider, and from then on that provider account signs them in. Someone the server doesn't know is told to ask the Owner for an invite.

## Sign-in protections

highsea slows down anyone guessing passwords:

- After 10 failed attempts at one account within 15 minutes, passwords for that account are refused for 5 minutes.
- After 50 failed attempts from one address within 15 minutes, that address is refused for 5 minutes.
- Each sign-in allows 5 tries at a two-step code, and the code step expires after 5 minutes.

Passkeys and single sign-on keep working during a pause, so someone guessing can't lock you out. Restarting highsea clears the counts.
