Written for highsea 0.1.0.

**Settings › Appearance** sets how the server presents itself to everyone who connects: its name, mark and accent, the theme the apps start in, and where interface sounds play. The Owner and Admins can change it.

Most of what's here is a default for highsea's apps, which each person can change on their own device. The admin itself keeps highsea's own look; it shows the server's name in the sidebar, on the sign-in page and on invite links.

The identity, the default theme and the sounds save together with **Save**, and every app picks them up the next time it checks in. Custom themes save on their own.

## Server identity

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | The server's name, up to 40 characters. Shown in the sidebar, on the sign-in page ("Sign in to" and the name) and to people following an invite ("Welcome to" and the name). It can't be empty. |
| **Mark** | A square SVG or PNG, at least 256 px and at most 1 MB, that apps show beside the name. **Use the highsea mark** goes back to highsea's own. |
| **Use a brand accent** | One accent colour for every theme. Off, each theme keeps its own. |

**In the apps** shows the name and mark as the apps' server switcher will show them.

When you pick an accent, highsea checks its contrast. **Passes contrast** means it's used as it is; **Adjusted for contrast** means highsea adjusts its shades so text on it stays readable. **Not a color** means the value isn't one highsea can read: use a hex value such as `#8fbfb7`.

## Themes

The apps start in the default theme, which you choose here, and anyone can pick another on their own device. [Themes](/settings/appearance/themes/) covers the built-in themes and making your own.

## Sounds

![The Sounds group: switches for TVs, Phones and tablets, and Web and desktop, then Volume, Sound pack and a Listen button for each sound.](/images/admin/settings-appearance/sounds.png)

Where the apps play their interface sounds until someone changes it on their device. Sounds never play over a film.

| Setting | Default |
| --- | --- |
| **TVs** | On: sound confirms each press of the remote. |
| **Phones and tablets** | Off: phones have haptics. |
| **Web and desktop** | Off. |
| **Volume** | 80% |
| **Sound pack** | Felt and wood. More packs come with the catalog. |

**Listen** has a button for each sound (Move, Select, Back, Done, Error, Notice, Arrival). Each plays at the chosen volume, even where sounds are off.

## Your own dashboard

Each person arranges their own dashboard in the admin with **Customize**, and the Owner sets the layout Admins start from. [Dashboard](/dashboard/customize/) explains how.
