Written for highsea 0.1.0.

Without a forwarded port, downloads still work: highsea connects out to peers. With one, peers can connect in too, so more peers can reach you, which helps with less popular torrents and with seeding.

Torrents use the forwarded port while **Use the forwarded port** is on, which it is by default. It's under [Behaviour](/settings/vpn/#behaviour).

## By provider

- **Proton VPN** and **Private Internet Access**: highsea asks the provider for a port once connected, keeps it renewed, and moves torrents to it. If the provider doesn't forward one, highsea tries again every minute and the check warns.
- **AirVPN**, **Windscribe** and **Another WireGuard provider**: reserve a port with your provider and type it into **Forwarded port**. Leave the field empty if there isn't one.
- **Mullvad**, **IVPN** and **Surfshark** don't forward ports.
- **A VPN container**: highsea uses the port gluetun reports. Turn it on in gluetun; see [A VPN container](/settings/vpn/container/#port-forwarding).
- **An interface on this machine**: forward the port through your tunnel yourself, if your provider allows it.

For AirVPN, Windscribe and another provider, the field is under the configuration file:

![highsea connects with AirVPN chosen: its steps, which end with reserving a port under Client Area › Forwarded ports, then WireGuard configuration and an empty Forwarded port field marked Optional](/images/admin/settings-vpn/forwarded-port.png)

## Checking it

The **Port forwarding** check on the [VPN page](/settings/vpn/#status-and-checks) shows the forwarded port and how many peers have connected in, or why there isn't one. Your home router's port, and **Open the port with UPnP** in [Download client](/settings/downloads/connections/#upnp-and-nat-pmp), don't help while a VPN is in use.
