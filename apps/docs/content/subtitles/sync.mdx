Written for highsea 0.1.0.

A subtitle made for another release of the same film is often early or late by a second or more. highsea listens for speech in the film and lines the subtitle up with it, in the background, after each download.

Each sync reports how sure it is, as a percentage:

- At or above **Minimum confidence**, the new timing is used and the track reads **Synced**, with the correction in milliseconds and the confidence.
- Below it, highsea keeps the subtitle's original timing and the track reads **Not synced**, with its confidence, so you can check it yourself.

![Nosferatu's Subtitles tab: an English subtitle from OpenSubtitles.com with a score of 99, Not synced at 0%, and its offset at 0 ms with − and + beside it.](/images/admin/subtitles/film-not-synced.png)

Syncing needs FFmpeg. If it's missing, every sync fails at once and **System › Health** warns: "ffmpeg isn't installed, so subtitles can't be synced to the audio." The official image includes it. Subtitles that are pictures rather than text can't be synced.

## Sync again

Choose **Sync to the audio again** on a track. It goes back to **Waiting to sync**, any offset you set is cleared, and the result arrives in a moment.

## Adjust the offset by hand

If a track is still a little early or late, use **−** and **+** beside its offset. Each press moves it by 50 ms: **−** shows the subtitles earlier, **+** later. The file is rewritten at once, so the change shows the next time the film is played.

## Scoring and sync settings

The **Scoring and sync** tab on the **Subtitles** page holds the settings for every film. Change them, then choose **Save**, or **Discard changes** to go back.

![The Scoring and sync tab: Minimum score 80, Prefer release matches and Sync to the audio on, Minimum confidence 70%, and Keep upgrading for 14 days.](/images/admin/subtitles/scoring.png)

| Setting | What it does | Starts at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Minimum score** | Subtitles below this score, from 0 to 100, are never downloaded. A profile can ask for more, not less. | 80 |
| **Prefer release matches** | A subtitle made for this exact release beats one matched only on title and year. | On |
| **Sync to the audio** | Every downloaded subtitle is lined up with the speech. Off, subtitles keep their original timing and read **Not synced**. | On |
| **Minimum confidence** | Below this, highsea keeps the original timing. Choose from 50% to 90%. | 70% |
| **Keep upgrading for** | How many days after import highsea keeps looking for a better match, up to 365. | 14 days |
