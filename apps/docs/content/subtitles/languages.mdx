Written for highsea 0.1.0.

A language profile says which subtitles a film should have. highsea starts with one called **English**. Every film has a profile, chosen under **Subtitle languages**:

- when you add a film;
- in a film's **Edit** dialog;
- for several films at once, from the **Subtitle languages** menu in the library's selection bar;
- as a default for every film added to a root folder, in [Media management](/settings/media-management/root-folders/#subtitle-languages-for-films-added-here).

![The Languages tab, with a card for each profile listing its languages, its cutoff and its minimum score, and New profile below.](/images/admin/subtitles/languages.png)

## Make or change a profile

1. Open **Subtitles**, then the **Languages** tab.
2. Choose **New profile**, or **Edit** on an existing one. The card turns into a form.
3. Give it a **Name**.
4. Under **Languages, in order of preference**, choose **Add a language** for each one you want. Drag a language by its handle to reorder it, or press Space on the handle, move it with the Up and Down arrows, and press Space again to drop it.
5. For each language, choose which kinds to fetch:
   - **Regular only**, **Regular and forced** or **Forced only**. Forced subtitles cover only the lines in another language, such as a sign or a short scene. **Regular and forced** fetches both, as two tracks.
   - **Without SDH**, **With or without SDH** or **SDH only**. SDH subtitles also describe sounds and name the speaker, for viewers who are deaf or hard of hearing. With **Without SDH**, highsea takes an SDH subtitle only when nothing else exists, and removes the descriptions from it.
6. Set the **Cutoff**: once this language is found, highsea stops looking for better matches. **Keep looking** never stops.
7. Set a **Minimum score** from 0 to 100. A match on the file itself scores about 99.
8. Choose **Save profile**.

![The English profile open for editing: its name, English as the first language with Regular and forced and Without SDH, Add a language, the cutoff, the minimum score of 80, and Save profile, Cancel and Delete.](/images/admin/subtitles/profile-edit.png)

## Delete a profile

**Delete** removes a profile. highsea refuses while films still use it, and always keeps at least one.
