Written for highsea 0.1.0.

highsea finds subtitles for every film it imports, scores them, and syncs them to the film's speech in the background. The **Subtitles** page shows how that's going, lists what's still missing, and holds the settings behind it. A film's own subtitles are on its page, under **Subtitles**.

Owners and Admins can use everything here, except adding a provider's account, which is for the Owner only.

## The page at a glance

![The Subtitles page, with today's figures across the top, the Wanted, Providers, Languages and Scoring and sync tabs, and Nosferatu on the wanted list, missing French.](/images/admin/subtitles/overview.png)

Across the top, four figures:

- **synced today**: subtitles lined up with the speech since midnight UTC.
- **waiting to sync**: subtitles queued or being synced now.
- **average correction**: how far today's syncs moved the timing, in milliseconds.
- **average confidence**: how sure today's syncs were.

Below them are four tabs: **Wanted**, **Providers**, **Languages** and **Scoring and sync**. The [Providers](/subtitles/providers/) and [Languages](/subtitles/languages/) tabs have pages of their own; **Scoring and sync** is covered with [syncing](/subtitles/sync/#scoring-and-sync-settings).

## The wanted list

The **Wanted** tab lists the films that are missing a language their profile asks for, with the missing languages beside each. Subtitles inside the video file count, so a film with built-in English isn't missing English. Films show 50 at a time; **Show more** loads the next.

- **Search** on a row searches every provider for that film's missing languages and downloads the best match.
- **Search all** does the same for every film on the list. What's found downloads and syncs in the background.

When nothing is missing, the tab says "Every movie has its subtitles".

## Automatic downloads

You don't need to search by hand. highsea:

- searches for a film's subtitles as soon as it's imported;
- searches the wanted list again every 6 hours;
- keeps looking for a better match for the number of days in **Keep upgrading for**, checking every 12 hours, and replaces a subtitle when it finds a better one.

A subtitle that came with the release, beside the video or in a `Subs` folder, is kept and scores 100, since it was made for that release. Subtitles inside the video file are used as they are.

Before saving a downloaded subtitle, highsea removes advertising lines, such as "Subtitles by …" or a site's address.

Both searches are listed under **System › Tasks** as **Search for wanted subtitles** and **Upgrade subtitles**, with **Run now** to start one early. See [System](/system/).

## Sidecar files

highsea saves each subtitle as a file beside the video, named after it, so players such as Plex, Jellyfin and Kodi find it. The name carries the language and, when there is one, a flag:

```txt
Sintel (2010) Bluray-1080p.mkv
Sintel (2010) Bluray-1080p.en.srt
Sintel (2010) Bluray-1080p.en.forced.srt
Sintel (2010) Bluray-1080p.en.sdh.srt
```

A second track with the same language and flag adds the provider's name, such as `….en.OpenSubtitles.srt`. When highsea renames a film, its subtitles are renamed with it.
