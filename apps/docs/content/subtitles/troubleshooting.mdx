Written for highsea 0.1.0.

## What System › Health says

**System › Health** lists subtitle problems under the source **Subtitles**:

- **… refused the password. Sign in again in Subtitles.** or **… refused the key. Check it in Subtitles.**: open **Edit** on the provider and enter the account again. See [Add an account](/subtitles/providers/#add-an-account).
- **… has no downloads left today.**: a notice. The provider is skipped until its downloads come back, at the time shown.
- **ffmpeg isn't installed**: every sync fails at once until FFmpeg is there. The official image includes it. See [System](/system/).

## A provider is waiting

On the **Providers** tab, a provider whose bar has emptied is waiting, and its line says why and when it's back. Searches skip it until then. [Requests left today](/subtitles/providers/#requests-left-today) lists each reason.

## A search finds nothing

If no provider is switched on, a film's search says "Turn on a subtitle provider in Subtitles to search." A provider can only be switched on once its account is added, by the Owner. See [Providers and accounts](/subtitles/providers/).

## A track reads Not synced or Sync failed

**Not synced** means the sync was less sure than **Minimum confidence**, so highsea kept the subtitle's original timing. Play the film and check it. If it's off, [sync it again](/subtitles/sync/#sync-again), or [move the offset by hand](/subtitles/sync/#adjust-the-offset-by-hand).

**Sync failed** on every track usually means FFmpeg is missing; **System › Health** says so. Subtitles that are pictures rather than text can't be synced.
